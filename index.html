Maya’s Halloween week

This week has been unusual. Last Saturday, I bought orange paper at the market and spent the evening cutting out bats. Since then, I’ve already decorated my house and I’ve just made a Halloween lantern. However, I haven’t carved a pumpkin yet.

Yesterday, my friend called me after school. She has invited me to a Halloween party. She has already chosen her costume, but I haven’t decided what to wear yet. When I was ten, I wore a vampire costume and won a small prize, but I’ve never dressed up as a witch before. Maybe I can try it this year!

Have you ever visited a haunted house? I haven’t, but I’ve recently watched several videos about haunted places. They have been quite scary! So far, I’ve watched two horror films, but I haven’t seen a ghost.
